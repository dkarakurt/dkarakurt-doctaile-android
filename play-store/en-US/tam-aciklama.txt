Whatever is in your bar, a cocktail from it.

doctaile looks at the bottles you already have and picks a cocktail you can actually make with them. No missing ingredients: exact measures in ml, step-by-step method, glass and garnish.

HOW IT WORKS
• Sign in with your email. No password; your profile stays on your phone.
• 156 ingredients on 17 shelves: whiskies, vermouths, liqueurs, syrups, fresh herbs… Pick what you have, or add your own.
• Tap Make or shake your phone like a shaker. The glass fills on screen while the phone vibrates in shaker rhythm.
• Read the recipe: measures, method, glass, garnish and source.

217 RECIPES
From IBA official classics (Negroni, Old Fashioned, Margarita, Espresso Martini…) to tiki drinks, spritzes and Turkish twists. Every recipe is labelled: IBA classic, adaptation or house improvisation.

LITTLE DETAILS
• No repeats: you won't see the same cocktail again until every drink your ingredients allow has been shown.
• Favourites: save recipes you like and make them again with one tap.
• Level liquid: tilt the phone on a recipe and the drink stays level.
• Alcohol-free mode with mocktails.
• Book mode on Galaxy Z Fold and tablets: shelves on the left, bar on the right.
• Fully offline: no internet permission, no ads, no data collection.

Recipes with alcohol are for ages 18+. Profiles under 18 only see alcohol-free recipes. Please drink responsibly and never drink and drive. (The app interface is in Turkish.)
